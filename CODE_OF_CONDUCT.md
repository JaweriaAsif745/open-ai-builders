# Code of Conduct 🤝

## Our Pledge

At Open AI Builders, we are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or technical expertise.

We believe open-source communities grow stronger when people feel safe asking questions, sharing ideas, learning from mistakes, and helping one another.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Welcoming beginners and helping them learn.
- Giving feedback that improves the work without attacking the person.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Acknowledging contributors and crediting original work.
- Accepting feedback and learning from mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, bullying, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, humiliating, or deliberately intimidating others.
- Trolling, spamming, or disrupting discussions.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Plagiarism or knowingly misrepresenting someone else's work.
- Any other conduct that makes participation hostile or unsafe.

## Our Responsibilities

Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards fairly.

They may remove inappropriate content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness inappropriate behavior, contact the repository owner through GitHub or use the repository's available reporting channels.

Please provide relevant details where possible. Reports will be handled respectfully and as confidentially as reasonably possible.

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Open AI Builders' repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces officially representing the community.

## Our Commitment

We want Open AI Builders to be a place where people can learn, collaborate, contribute meaningful work, and grow together.

Every member shares responsibility for maintaining a respectful and constructive environment.

Thank you for helping us build a better community! 🌱
